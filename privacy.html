Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Unwind is an Android app published by TaveroPrime. Contact: taveroprime@gmail.com.

Thoughts you enter

Text entered into Unwind is processed on your device for the dissolve animation. Unwind does not transmit your thoughts to TaveroPrime or save them as a history. The app clears entered text after the interaction and when it goes into the background.

Data collection and sharing

Unwind does not require an account or internet access and does not include advertising or analytics services. TaveroPrime does not collect or share personal information through the app.

Settings and notifications

App preferences, including sound, haptics, dissolve duration, and reminder settings, are stored locally on your device. If you enable daily reminders, Android may display local notifications. You can disable reminders in the app or your device settings.

Third-party services

When you download Unwind from Google Play, Google may process information under its own privacy policy. That processing is separate from Unwind's in-app functionality.

Children

Unwind is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The latest version will appear at this address.
